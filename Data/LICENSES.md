# Shipped data

`VendorSeed.lua` is generated by `tools/gen-vendor-seed.lua` (see its header for the inputs and
the command). Its prices come from WoW: Forever's own ItemSparse table (BuyPrice and
VendorStackCount), exported by wago.tools for product `wowf`. Which items a vendor sells comes
from two lists; only their item ids (and, from CMaNGOS, whether every vendor row limits the
stock) are used, no prices:

| Source | What is used | Licence | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LibPeriodicTable-3.1, set `Tradeskill.Mat.BySource.Vendor` (github.com/doadin/libperiodictable-3-1, commit 130c6a9) | item ids | LGPL-2.1 | `LICENSE-LibPeriodicTable-LGPL-2.1.txt` |
| CMaNGOS classic-db, tables `npc_vendor` and `npc_vendor_template` (github.com/cmangos/classic-db, commit 04bc476, dump z2815) | item ids, limited stock | GPL-3.0 | `LICENSE-CMaNGOS-classic-db-GPL-3.0.md` |

The approach (ItemSparse BuyPrice joined with a vendor list) follows CraftWise
(`tools/gen_vendor.py`, GPL-3.0) and SkillUp Forever (GPL-3.0-or-later); the generator is a fresh
implementation and copies no code from either.
